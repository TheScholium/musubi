Musubi is a Zettelkasten for **mathematics, physics and philosophy**. Each note holds one idea. Links between notes make the knowledge. The program is one HTML file: no server, no account, no network.

The name *Musubi* (結び) means a knot or a tie, from the verb *musubu*, to tie or bind. That is what links do here.

## A daily loop

1. **Capture** fast. Press <kbd>Alt C</kbd>. The thought goes to the *Inbox* tab as a fleeting note.
2. **Process** the inbox. Rewrite each thought in your own words, give it a type, and link it to at least one note. Then set its status to *Permanent*.
3. **Connect.** Type `[[` to link a note. If you want to name the relation, write a word first, such as `supports::[[…]]`. Look at *Unlinked mentions* at the foot of each note.
4. **Review.** Press <kbd>Alt R</kbd>. Named definitions and theorems become flashcards without extra work.
5. **Maintain.** The *Maintain* tab lists orphans and links to notes that do not exist yet.

> [!tip] Keyboard first
> <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> (or <kbd>⌘ K</kbd>) finds any note. Type `>` to run a command. Press <kbd>?</kbd> for all shortcuts.

## Your data

Notes are plain Markdown files with YAML front matter. They live in this browser until you **link a folder** (Settings → Data) or **export** them. Musubi also reads Obsidian vaults: drop the folder or a ZIP on the window.

These are example notes. Delete them when you are ready; they come back only if you add them again from Settings, Data.
