## Summary (in my own words)

A three-page paper. It states the JTB analysis in three forms (including Chisholm's and Ayer's), then gives two counterexamples. Both cases use two principles: justification can attach to falsehoods, and justification passes through deduction.

## Key claims

- The JTB conditions are not jointly sufficient for knowledge.
- See [[Gettier problem]] for the argument in standard form.

## My response

The cases are strong, but they depend on a fixed idea of "justification". A reliabilist account of justification changes the picture.
