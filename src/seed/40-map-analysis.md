A line of thought from covers to extreme values.

1. [[Open cover]]: the basic object.
2. [[Compact space]]: every open cover has a finite subcover.
3. [[Heine–Borel theorem]]: in $\R^n$, compact means closed and bounded.
4. [[Continuous image of a compact set is compact]]
5. [[Extreme value theorem]]: the payoff for optimisation.

## Open questions

- Sequential compactness and when it agrees with compactness.
- Tychonoff's theorem and the axiom of choice.
