The action principle seems to say the particle "chooses" its whole path using the end point. Does this mean the future fixes the present?

Things to check:

- The Euler–Lagrange form is local and causal: position and velocity now fix the future.
- The path is stationary, not minimal. "Least" is a misleading word.
- In quantum mechanics, the path-integral picture says all paths contribute and the classical path is where their phases agree.
